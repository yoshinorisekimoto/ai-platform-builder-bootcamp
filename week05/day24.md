# Day 24 — Protecting Revenue Without Lowering Product Quality

## Objective

Practice translating product and technical risks into commercial language and gaining Sales support.

## Scenario

A partner integration could generate $8M, but testing shows repeated mapping errors, incorrect job content, and unstable API implementation.

## Recommendation

Do not prioritize or continue the integration until the partner meets clear quality standards.

Set a time-bound recovery plan covering:

- root cause and ownership
- correction deadlines
- mapping and content accuracy
- monitoring and escalation
- suspension criteria

## Working with Sales

Product and Partner teams should own the quality requirements, partner communication, and recovery plan.

Sales should support the commercial case, internal escalation, and alignment with the partner’s commercial decision-makers.

The message to Sales should follow this sequence:

**Revenue risk → Product risk → Recovery plan → Sales support needed**

## Trade-off

Suspending the integration may delay short-term revenue.

However, continuing with inaccurate job information could increase support costs, damage job-seeker trust, and weaken long-term partner value.

## Key Learning

A strong Product decision is not enough.

Product leaders must translate quality risks into revenue impact and give Sales a practical way to help protect the deal.

## Plain-English Summary

Product protects quality. Sales helps secure commercial commitment.

Both teams should work together to fix the problem without lowering the standard.
