# Day 26 — AI Pilot, Commercial Momentum, and Platform Safety

## Objective
Practice balancing AI product risk, partner momentum, and commercial commitments.

## Scenario
A strategic partner wanted to expand an AI feature from pilot to GA.

Key evidence included:
- 95–97% AI accuracy
- No major complaints
- Remaining critical issues requiring Engineering validation
- $3M expansion opportunity

## Recommendation
Move forward with the partner, but do not compromise platform safety.

Use controlled rollout stages, complete required guardrail improvements, and commit to a clear GA timeline only when Engineering can support it.

## Commercial Translation
Technical progress should translate into:

**Evidence → Timeline → Partner confidence → Revenue protection**

Sales can maintain commercial momentum without forcing an unsafe launch.

## Stakeholder Buy-in
- Engineering gets enough time to fix and validate critical issues.
- The partner receives a clear roadmap and commitment.
- Sales can protect the expansion opportunity.
- Product maintains platform quality and safety.

## Point of View
**We should move fast, but not at the expense of platform safety.**

A senior partnerships leader should make clear not only what to do, but also what principle should not be compromised.

## Key Learning
Good judgment is not enough.

The decision should be supported by evidence, translated into commercial impact, and expressed with a clear point of view.
