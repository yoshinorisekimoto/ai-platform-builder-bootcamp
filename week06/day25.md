# Day 25 — Turning Product Constraints into a Commercial Plan

## Objective

Practice translating product and technical constraints into a commercial plan that Sales can act on.

## Scenario

A partner expansion could generate an additional $4M this quarter.

However:

* the API has an 8% error rate
* some issues can be fixed by the partner
* a remaining platform edge case requires internal Engineering
* a full-scope safe launch would take eight weeks
* a limited-scope launch could take five to six weeks

## Recommendation

Prioritize platform safety.

If the limited-scope option can be delivered safely in five to six weeks, choose that option.

The missed quarterly revenue target should be managed internally rather than influencing the partner decision.

## Translating for Sales

Product teams should explain not only what needs to change, but why it matters commercially.

**Technical action → Timeline → Launch probability → Revenue impact**

The key question is:

**So what for Sales?**

## Role Alignment

* Product / Engineering own technical feasibility and platform safety
* Platform Partnerships connects technical decisions to commercial impact
* Sales supports the commercial case and executive alignment

## Key Learning

A good Product decision is not enough.

Senior partnership leaders must translate technical constraints into timeline and revenue impact so that Sales can act on the information.

## Plain-English Summary

Keep the platform safe first.

Then explain how the technical plan affects timing and revenue. Internal sales targets should not drive decisions that create unnecessary partner or platform risk.
