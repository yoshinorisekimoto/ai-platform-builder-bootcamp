# Day 28 — Executive Communication Under Ambiguity

## Objective
Practice making clear executive recommendations while balancing commercial opportunity, platform stability, and technical uncertainty.

## Scenario
A strategic partner expansion created a $6M revenue opportunity.

Key evidence included:
- 94% pilot success rate
- No critical issues
- Monitoring detection time: around 20 minutes
- Two additional weeks could reduce detection time to under 5 minutes
- The partner's quarterly budget required the commercial agreement to close this week
- The technical launch did not need to happen in the same week

Later, competitive pressure put approximately $1.5M of the opportunity at risk if the launch was delayed by another week.

## Recommendation
Separate the commercial commitment from the technical launch.

Close the agreement when the commercial requirement is clear, while giving Engineering enough time to improve platform stability.

However, when new evidence shows that the remaining operational risk is manageable and the commercial impact becomes material, accelerate the launch.

## Evidence-Based Trade-off
The decision changed as the evidence changed.

Initially:

**More monitoring stability → Better partner trust**

Later:

**10-minute detection risk vs. $1.5M commercial opportunity**

With no critical incidents, rollback capability, and Engineering confirming production readiness, the remaining operational risk was acceptable.

## Executive Communication
Senior communication should clearly state the recommendation and the principle behind it.

Examples:

**A stable platform builds trust with our partners.**

**Before changing the plan, I need to understand the business rationale for accelerating to one week.**

After reviewing the additional evidence:

**I believe the current operational risk is manageable. The commercial impact is greater than the incremental monitoring risk.**

## Point of View
Do not choose speed or safety automatically.

Understand the severity of the risk, quantify the business impact, and make the trade-off explicitly.

## Human Review / Ownership
- Engineering owns technical readiness and operational risk assessment.
- Sales provides commercial impact and timing evidence.
- Platform Partnerships evaluates the combined trade-off and makes the recommendation clear to executives and partners.
- Human judgment remains responsible for the final decision.

## Key Learning
Senior presence is not about defending the same position regardless of new information.

A strong leader can change the recommendation when the evidence changes while keeping the decision principle consistent.

## Plain-English Summary
First understand how dangerous the technical risk really is.

Then compare it with the business impact.

If the technical risk is small and manageable but waiting creates a large business loss, moving faster can be the better decision.
