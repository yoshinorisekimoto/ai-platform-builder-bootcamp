# AI Platform Builder Bootcamp — Roadmap

## North Star

Build a practical operating model for how AI agents, partners, platforms, and human decision-makers can work together.

The goal is not to automate human judgment.

**AI handles evidence, routine coordination, and structured analysis.  
Humans own judgment, commitments, exceptions, risk acceptance, and relationships.**

All prototypes use synthetic data and are designed with future real-world deployment in mind.

---

## Milestones

| Milestone | Goal | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 30** | **Agentic Partnership Architecture** | Define how Agency Agents, Platform Agents, and Human Partner Leads should divide responsibilities. |
| **Day 40** | **Rules / Human-AI Boundary** | Create clear rules for what AI can do independently, when it must stop, and when human approval is required. |
| **Day 50** | **End-to-End Partner Workflow** | Let AI handle a partner request from evidence collection through decision preparation, while humans own the decision gate. Apply minimum-information and minimum-permission principles so the agent only receives what it needs. |
| **Day 60** | **Agent-to-Agent Prototype** | Enable a synthetic Agency Agent and Platform Agent to exchange requirements, evidence, and clarification questions before escalating exceptions to a human. |
| **Day 70** | **Trust / Permission / Governance** | Add permissions, confidence thresholds, approval gates, audit trails, and controls that prevent agents from making unauthorized commitments. |
| **Day 80** | **Cross-Industry Stress Test** | Test the operating model across different platform environments such as Digital Advertising, Commerce, and Fintech. |
| **Day 90** | **Agentic Partnership OS v1** | Connect request intake, agent-to-agent coordination, evidence analysis, human decision, partner response, executive communication, and audit trail into one working prototype. |
| **Day 120** | **Real-World Deployment Blueprint** | Define how the prototype could safely move into a real company environment, including security, privacy, access control, observability, governance, rollout, and business KPIs. |

---

## Continuous Tracks

Every milestone continues to develop three capabilities:

### 1. Platform Partnerships Judgment
Make sound decisions across partner value, revenue, product standards, engineering constraints, platform risk, and long-term ecosystem health.

### 2. Human-AI Operating Model
Determine what AI should execute, what information it should receive, and where human judgment must remain mandatory.

### 3. Executive Communication
Translate complex evidence into a clear recommendation, trade-off, commitment, and point of view.

---

## Design Principle

**Build → Decide → Communicate**

1. Build AI-assisted workflows.
2. Make the final judgment as a Platform Partnerships Lead.
3. Communicate the decision clearly to partners and executives.

The prototype should always answer one practical question:

**Could this workflow eventually be used in real partnership work without giving AI more information, authority, or responsibility than it needs?**
